CASE FILE: In re: Dispute between Rajesh Yadav and Dinesh Yadav

FILED: 12 March 2019, District Court, Kanpur

BACKGROUND:
Rajesh Yadav and Dinesh Yadav are two brothers disputing ownership of
a residential plot inherited from their late father. Rajesh claims a
verbal partition arrangement was made in 2017.

ORDERS/HEARINGS:
- 12 March 2019: Case filed by Rajesh Yadav.
- 8 July 2019: First hearing; Dinesh filed counter-affidavit.
- 22 January 2021: Court ordered joint site inspection.
- 15 September 2022: Commissioner's report submitted.

CURRENT STATUS: Case pending final judgment.
